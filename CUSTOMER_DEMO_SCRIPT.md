# Customer demonstration script — 8 to 10 minutes

## 1. Executive Overview — 2 minutes

“JSAN has built a controlled cross-provider POI comparison workflow. In the Denpasar CORE benchmark, we compared 400 Grab POIs against 562 Google POIs.”

Point out:
- 53 matched pairs
- 39 Tier-1 direct-probe candidates
- 139 Tier-2 area-supported leads
- zero customer-release records until validation is complete

## 2. Comparison Proof — 2 minutes

Open a high-confidence match and explain that the decision uses business name, address and coordinate distance. Possible and unresolved records are deliberately excluded from the non-Google claim.

## 3. Priority Sample — 2 minutes

Open one Strong 10 candidate. Say:

“This record has direct-probe evidence and precise source coordinates. It is a priority validation candidate, not yet a released salon. The next gates are category, contact, activity, full Google exclusion and independent QA.”

## 4. Coverage Map — 1 minute

Show matched, Tier 1 and Tier 2 layers. Explain that the map uses actual imported coordinates and that the Google collection stopped at the approved budget, so this is a controlled CORE-area benchmark rather than a city total.

## 5. Cost & Scale — 1 minute

Explain that API consumption is modest and controlled through call limits and field masks. The main effort is research, evidence and QA—not API calls.

## 6. Customer Pack — 1 to 2 minutes

Download the executive workbook. Request approval for a Strong 10/25 validation sprint and agreement on:
- accepted salon categories
- mandatory customer fields
- evidence format
- Gold/Silver acceptance threshold
- five-city scale plan

## Close

“The current POC proves the discovery and comparison method. The next step is to prove a small number of records deeply, then scale the accepted method across the target cities.”
